# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. We are here to build a deployment platform, not to win
arguments.

## What we expect

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings about context.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers have to say no sometimes, and that a slow reply is
  usually a busy week rather than a snub.
- Respect that contributors differ in experience, language, background and time
  zone.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.
- Encouraging any of the above.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, discussions and any
other space where you are acting on behalf of a Nomploy project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to [contact@nomploy.com](mailto:contact@nomploy.com). Reports
are handled privately, and we will not disclose the reporter's identity to the
person reported.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may
warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour warrants it.
We aim to be proportionate: a first misstep usually gets a private word, not a
ban.
